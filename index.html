Client · Commerce

Desi Totes

Cotton canvas totes, cut and printed after the order arrives. Nothing sits in a warehouse, so nothing on the page can be vague.

Fabric weight, finished size, zip or no zip and the price gap between them all land before anyone reaches a cart. It takes cards through Razorpay, switches currency in place and keeps a bag that survives a refresh.

Both ends, or it's not finished

A shop that renders beautifully and loses a cart is not a shop. We build the surface and the thing underneath it, and we stay on afterwards.

  • Interface

    React and Vite, Tailwind for the system, GSAP where scroll has to carry meaning. Real markup, keyboard order, reduced motion honoured.

  • Service

    Node and Express over MongoDB: catalogue, cart, orders and the admin side of them. Razorpay for cards, with the states that go wrong handled.

  • Running

    A content security policy, image budgets, lazy media and a deploy that doesn't need anyone awake. Then fixing it in production.
